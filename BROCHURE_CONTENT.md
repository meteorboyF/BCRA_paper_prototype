# PangoChain Brochure Content

## Project Name

PangoChain

## One-Line Description

PangoChain is an AI-powered blockchain legal platform for secure case, document, evidence, and audit management.

## Problem Statement

Legal teams handle sensitive documents, client communication, evidence, deadlines, and compliance records across disconnected tools. This creates risks: document tampering, unclear access history, slow review, weak audit trails, and poor client visibility.

## What PangoChain Does

PangoChain brings legal case management, encrypted document storage, AI assistance, and blockchain-backed audit trails into one secure platform.

## Core Value

- Protect confidential legal documents.
- Prove document integrity and chain of custody.
- Help lawyers review evidence faster with AI.
- Give clients clear visibility into their cases.
- Give firm leaders audit and compliance oversight.

## Lawyer-Side Features

- Case and matter management.
- Encrypted document upload and secure download.
- Document version history and chain of custody.
- Role-based document access control.
- AI legal chat with selected case documents.
- AI document risk analysis.
- AI evidence-gap and timeline review.
- AI hearing preparation.
- AI legal draft generation.
- E-signature and signing workflows.
- Redaction and annotation tools.
- Billing, settlement, reminders, and team communication.

## Client-Side Features

- Secure client portal.
- Case status overview in plain language.
- Client document vault.
- Secure document access.
- Client reminders and updates.
- Privacy and deletion request tools.
- AI client assistant for simple case guidance.

## Managing Partner / Admin Features

- Audit trail dashboard.
- Security alerts.
- Document access monitoring.
- Compliance reporting.
- User and firm management.
- Regulator-ready activity records.

## AI Features

- Case-aware legal assistant.
- Document summarization.
- Contract and clause risk detection.
- Evidence-gap identification.
- Timeline inconsistency checks.
- Hearing prep briefs.
- Legal draft generation.
- Client-friendly case explanations.

## Blockchain Features

- Hyperledger Fabric-backed audit trail.
- Tamper-evident document hash records.
- IPFS CID anchoring.
- Access grant and revoke records.
- Document version proof.
- Chain-of-custody support.

## Security Features

- Browser-side document encryption workflow.
- Encrypted IPFS document storage.
- PostgreSQL-backed application state.
- JWT authentication.
- MFA support.
- Per-document access control.
- Audit logs for sensitive actions.

## Short Pitch

PangoChain helps law firms securely manage cases and documents while using AI to speed up legal work. Documents are encrypted, stored through IPFS, and anchored with blockchain proofs so lawyers, clients, and firm leaders can trust every action in the case lifecycle.

## Impact

- Saves lawyer time on document review and drafting.
- Reduces risk of document tampering or unclear access history.
- Improves client transparency.
- Strengthens compliance and audit readiness.
- Makes advanced legal technology easier to access.
