# Witness Statement - Maintenance Notice

Case: Chen v. Meridian Holdings - Contract Dispute  
Witness: Elena Ruiz, front desk concierge at Meridian Commercial Plaza  
Interviewed by: Priya Nair, Paralegal  
Interview date: May 21, 2026

## Statement Summary

I worked the front desk at Meridian Commercial Plaza from January 2024 through April 2026. I remember Marcus Chen and his team because they reported water coming through the ceiling above Suite 4B several times. The first in-person report I remember was January 18, 2024, around 8:20 a.m. Mr. Chen showed me water stains and a wet ceiling tile near the conference room.

On January 24, 2024, I saw a maintenance request marked "urgent" in the building portal for Suite 4B. The request referenced a ceiling leak, a wet router cabinet, and concern that client files could be damaged. I remember telling Mr. Chen that Meridian's property manager, Elaine Porter, had received the portal notice.

On February 3, 2024, Mr. Chen came to the desk again with photographs on his phone. He said the leak was worse after overnight rain. I called the maintenance radio line and asked whether a plumber had been scheduled. The technician on duty, Sam Patel, told me the request was still "waiting for owner approval."

## Key Facts

- Meridian had actual notice of the water intrusion before the February 12, 2024 termination notice.
- The building portal contained a January 24, 2024 urgent request from Suite 4B.
- The leak affected the conference room and the network cabinet area.
- Elaine Porter was the Meridian property manager receiving the notices.
- Maintenance delayed repair because the work order required owner approval.

## Potential Cross-Examination Risks

- I do not personally control the building portal logs.
- I did not print the January 24 request at the time.
- I rely on memory for the exact wording of the maintenance radio call.
