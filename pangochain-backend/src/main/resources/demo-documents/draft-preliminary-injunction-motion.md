# Draft Preliminary Injunction Motion

## Relief Requested

Marcus Chen requests an order preventing Meridian Holdings Ltd. from terminating the lease or interfering with access to Unit 14B pending resolution of the breach dispute.

## Factual Basis

Meridian failed to provide the ten-business-day cure period required by Section 4.2 of the lease. Meridian also failed to repair structural roof leaks after repeated written notices.

## Irreparable Harm

Chen Studio Works relies on the Riverside Arcade location for client consultations and product photography. Loss of the location would cause reputational harm, cancellation of booked client sessions, and loss of goodwill that cannot be fully compensated by damages.

## Evidence List

1. Executed lease agreement.
2. Termination notice.
3. Rent payment ledger.
4. Roof leak photographs.
5. Maintenance email chain.
