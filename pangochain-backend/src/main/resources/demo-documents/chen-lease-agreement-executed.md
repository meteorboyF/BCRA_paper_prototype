# Chen Lease Agreement - Executed

## Parties

This commercial lease agreement is entered into between Marcus Chen, trading as Chen Studio Works, and Meridian Holdings Ltd. for the premises at Unit 14B, Riverside Arcade.

## Premises And Term

The lease term began on 1 March 2023 and runs for thirty-six months. The premises may be used for design services, client consultations, and light product photography.

## Rent And Security Deposit

Monthly rent is USD 12,500, payable on the first business day of each month. A security deposit of USD 37,500 was paid by wire transfer on 27 February 2023.

## Maintenance Obligations

Meridian Holdings Ltd. is responsible for structural repairs, roof leaks, fire safety systems, and common-area access. Marcus Chen is responsible for ordinary interior maintenance and utilities.

## Notice

All breach notices must be delivered in writing with a ten-business-day cure period. Email notice alone is not sufficient unless acknowledged by both parties.

## Confidential Work Product Note

Counsel should compare the notice clause against Meridian's termination letter because the letter appears to omit the contractually required cure period.
