## Mitigation Steps

- Client moved sensitive paper files to offsite storage on January 24, 2024.
- Client migrated network services away from Suite 4B on January 25, 2024.
- Client used temporary meeting space beginning March 4, 2024.
- Client preserved email notices and photographs, but original maintenance portal exports remain missing.

## AI Demo Hooks

Ask the AI to identify missing proof. Strong expected gaps include storage invoice, CRM/calendar export, temporary office receipt, portal export, and photographs with metadata.

Ask the AI to summarize damages. The expected answer should connect the water intrusion notices to emergency storage, IT migration, lost meetings, disputed rent, and temporary office costs.
