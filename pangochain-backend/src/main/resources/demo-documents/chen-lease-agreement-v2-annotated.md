# Chen Lease Agreement - Executed v2 Annotated

## Annotation Summary

This annotated copy highlights sections relevant to the preliminary injunction motion.

## Key Clauses

- Section 4.2 requires written notice and a ten-business-day cure period.
- Section 8.1 requires landlord maintenance of roof and structural systems.
- Section 12.3 allows emergency injunctive relief when access to the premises is interrupted.

## Counsel Annotations

The strongest argument is not nonpayment. The stronger argument is that Meridian terminated without the required cure process while also failing to repair the roof leak that disrupted Chen Studio Works.

## Proposed Evidence

Attach the executed lease, payment ledger, maintenance emails, and photographs of the damaged ceiling. The paralegal team should verify dates against bank records before filing.
